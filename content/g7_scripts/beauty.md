# G7 Beauty 💅💄🦋
by **G7 Development** · https://g7-development.tebex.io/ · Discord: https://discord.gg/P6XQ4N6M95

The all-in-one beauty script for the ladies: nails, makeup and lashes, each with its own live studio.
Qbox, QBCore and ESX are auto-detected, as are g7_inventory, ox, qs, qb and the ESX inventory, and ox_target or qb-target.

**Nails**
- Live nail studio with 10 finishes, ombré, 6 French tips, 24 patterns, 25 charms, lettering, freehand brush, image import
- Nails wear and break: polish chips over time, and punches, climbing, falls or crashes can snap a nail. A Nail Repair Kit (or a new set) fixes them. `/nailcheck` shows their condition
- 12 Hot Sets plus seasonal drops (Spooky Season, Holiday Glam, New Year, Valentine's, Spring, Summer) with a Limited badge, saved designs, salon lookbook, press-ons, acetone, `/nails` and `/shownails` gestures

**Makeup** (makeup artist job)
- Eyeshadow & liner, blush & contour, lipstick with a second tone, brows, freckles, all with intensity + real GTA color palettes
- Colored contacts (32), lip filler / brow lift / cheek contour
- Face close-up camera with front / left / right / back angles and hold-to-compare before & after
- Makeup runs when you swim (or turn it waterproof), makeup remover wipes

**Lash Bar** (makeup artist job)
- 33 lash styles: a 7-piece photo collection made from real strip photos (Aurora, Delilah, Ivy, Lexi, Scarlett, Mariah, Rose), an 8-piece strip collection (Feather Wisp, 3D Mink Fluff, Spaced Spikes, Criss-Cross Mink, Full Drama, Flared Wispy, 25mm Dramatic, 5D Spike Fluff), plus Classic Set, Natural Hybrid, Natural Classic, Wispy Hybrid, Fluffy Hybrid, Spiky Wispy, Spiky Wet Set, Wet Look Spikes, Manga Lash, Fox Eye, Cat Eye, Dramatic Cat Eye, Doll Eye, Lash Lift Curl, Full Glam Strip, Russian Volume, Mink Volume, Mega Volume
- Real application on a close-up of the actual character: prime the lids, pick each piece with 3D tweezers, dip it in glue on a jade stone, place it on the lash line, seal with bonder mist
- Strip lashes (1 per eye), clusters (8 per eye) or individual lashes (16 per eye). Glue dries, strip glue has to get tacky, misplaced pieces can be pulled off
- The face shows only what's been placed so far. The full set only saves once every piece on both eyes is placed, sealed and paid for. Leave early and it comes off
- Uses lash tweezers, lash glue and strips / clusters / individual lashes as items. Makeup remover takes lashes off

**Beauty Supply shop**
- Built-in shop with a clerk and blip: buy nail kits, acetone, remover wipes, lash tools and lashes, and the vanity
- Same Y2K look as the studios, categories, quantity steppers, a bag with cash or card checkout. Every item weighs 1

**Salon**
- Two jobs (nail tech and makeup artist) with stations for each, including a lash bed, and a front desk kiosk
- Check-in queue: clients check in, techs get pinged and send them to a station
- Bills with tips, loyalty rewards (every Nth visit discounted) and gift cards
- Star reviews after every appointment, shown at the kiosk with the owner's reply
- Appointments: clients book a service, day and time at the kiosk. Techs see the day's schedule, claim bookings and get a ping 10 minutes before. Booked clients skip to the front of the line
- Owner dashboard: revenue graph, today / week / tips / rating, sales by service, hire nearby players (with an accept pop-up), promote, demote and fire, set suggested and minimum prices, tech leaderboard, reply to reviews, manage bookings and turn seasonal drops on or off
- Tech levels: XP per client, higher levels earn a bigger commission (`/beautystats`)
- Looks save straight into illenium-appearance / fivem-appearance / qb-clothing / esx_skin

**Placeable vanity**
- Use the item and place it anywhere with the built-in placement tool (aim, rotate, raise, nudge, snap to floor)
- Third-eye: do your nails / makeup / lashes, adjust placement, settings, collect earnings, pick it up
- Owners can open it to the public and charge a fee; admins can remove any vanity
